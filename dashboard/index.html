    Safety Checks (Enable / Disable)

    ▸ Stall Detection
    Triggers when:
    • PWM output ≥ 25%
    • AND actual velocity < 0.5 RPM
    • AND position error > 5° (in POSITION/GHOST mode)
    • Sustained for 2 seconds
    Cause: motor blocked by obstacle, mechanical jam, or wiring fault.
    ▸ Encoder Check
    Triggers when (either):
    • Phase inversion: PWM > 50% one way, RPM > 5 RPM the other way
    • Signal loss: PWM > 50%, RPM near 0, encoder counts frozen for 1 second
    Cause: encoder cable disconnected, A/B swapped, or hardware fault.
    ▸ Over-Rotation
    Triggers when:
    • Absolute position exceeds ±720° (2 full turns) from home
    Cause: wire-twist protection — prevents internal wiring from snapping.
    ▸ Joystick Check
    Triggers when:
    • ESP32 sends a packet with status byte indicating disconnected
    Cause: joystick power off, Bluetooth/wireless link lost, or ESP32 fault.
